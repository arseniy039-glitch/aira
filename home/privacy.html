Privacy policy

This page explains what happens to the information you submit through the request forms on Airahome, Aira Energia and Aira Garden (together, "Aira Home"), operated by Horizonte Solene Lda, Algarve, Portugal.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form: your name, phone number, and details of your request (address or location, a description of the problem or property, and how urgent it is). We do not collect payment information through these forms.

Why we collect it

Solely to respond to your request — to call or message you back, confirm a price, and arrange the visit or consultation you asked for.

Where it goes

Form submissions are sent directly to our internal request system and forwarded to our team so we can follow up. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing.

How long we keep it

For as long as needed to handle your request and, if you become a customer, for as long as required to provide the service. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you. Write to info@aira-ai.net and we will respond.
